Hotels in Addis Ababa

A curated list of quality, conference-friendly hotels, from the flagship Sheraton and airport-side Skylight to excellent upper-midrange options in Bole and Kazanchis.

Luxury & Five-Star

Flagship hotels

Addis Ababa's quality hotels cluster in three areas: Bole (near the airport), Kazanchis (the UN/diplomatic district), and the city center around Meskel Square. Every listing below includes a direct phone line, tap to call, and an email where available. Phone numbers are drawn from the hotels and the African Union's published Addis Ababa hotel directory; confirm details when you book.

Booking tips for ASOC 2026.

November is peak conference season in Addis, book early and confirm airport pickup with your hotel. If you prefer walking to sessions, wait for the official venue announcement before choosing your district. Rates vary by season; verify prices and numbers directly with hotels or major booking platforms.
