Register for ASOC 2026

Join clinicians, researchers, policymakers, and advocates from across Africa and the diaspora. ASOC 2026 is a hybrid conference, attend in person at the Adwa Memorial Museum in Addis Ababa or virtually from anywhere in the world. Before You Begin

What to know about registering

🎟️

No Registration Fee

There is no registration fee for the conference this year. Registration is required for all participants, whether attending in person or online.

🌍

Attend In Person or Virtually

ASOC 2026 is a hybrid event. Join us at the Adwa Memorial Museum in Addis Ababa from November 1 to 4, or participate virtually from anywhere in the world.

✉️

Invitation Letters

If you need an invitation letter for visa purposes, indicate this on the form and provide your passport full name, number, and expiration date.

Have the following ready:

Your name as it should appear on conference materials, country of residence, email, WhatsApp number, organization or institution, job title, professional category, and which days you plan to attend if joining in person. Questions? Email asocconference@gmail.com.
