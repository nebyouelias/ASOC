About the Conference

African science, African leadership, African solutions

Launched in 2021, the African Stroke Organization Conference is the first dedicated transcontinental annual scientific forum focused on cerebrovascular disease in Africa. It brings together key stakeholders to identify gaps in knowledge, set priorities for stroke prevention, care delivery, and research, and accelerate the translation of research findings into better stroke outcomes across the region.

ASOC 2026 marks a milestone: the conference comes to Addis Ababa, the diplomatic capital of Africa and seat of the African Union, held jointly with the 1st Ethiopia Stroke Foundation Comprehensive Stroke Symposium, under the theme "Enhancing Capacity, Expanding Impact: Stroke Care Transformation in Africa."

The Journey So Far

Five editions, four countries, one movement

Every year since 2021, ASOC has convened the continent's stroke community in the first week of November. What began as a virtual forum has grown into Africa's premier annual stroke congress, moving from West to Southern to East Africa, and now to the Horn.

Call for Abstract Submissions

Share your science in Addis Ababa

ASOC 2026 invites abstracts for poster or oral presentation. The conference welcomes submissions from researchers at all career stages, and particularly encourages early-career researchers, postdoctoral fellows, residents, graduate students, and trainees to submit and share their work. All accepted abstracts will be published in a peer-reviewed journal.

Submission rules

  1. Abstracts must be submitted in English.
  2. Use "ASOC 2026 Abstract - [Presenting Author Last Name]" as the subject line of the submission email.
  3. Submit ONE abstract per email. Multiple submissions on the same email might not be considered.
  4. Abstracts must be submitted as a Microsoft Word (.doc or .docx) document.
  5. Abstracts may be submitted for either poster or oral presentation.
  6. The ASOC Steering Committee will determine whether the abstract will be accepted for presentation.
  7. Abstracts must be received by the stated deadline; late abstracts will not be considered.
  8. Abstracts should be written in clear English with accurate grammar and spelling of publication quality. If you need help, arrange review by a native English-speaking colleague, a university publications office, or a copy editor before submitting.

Abstract structure

  1. Presenting author's contact details: full first and family name(s), email address, affiliation (department, institution/hospital, city, state if relevant, country), and phone number.
  2. Author and co-authors' details.
  3. Title in UPPER CASE, limited to 25 words. Submit symbols as words.
  4. Abstract text limited to 250 words including acknowledgements (tables affect the word count). Submit the text as a Word or PDF file.
  5. Abstracts should clearly state: Background and aims, Methods, Results, and Conclusions.
  6. Images: maximum 500 KB each, maximum 600(w) x 800(h) pixels, in JPG, GIF, or PNG format, sent in the same message as the abstract text.
  7. Use only standard abbreviations; place unusual abbreviations in parentheses after the full word at first use.
  8. Use generic drug names; presentations must be balanced with no commercial promotional content.
  9. No patient names, hospital ID numbers, or other identifying information.
  10. Changes are not possible after submission. Once received, the program coordinator will contact you to confirm receipt.

Please note

  • The same person may submit up to 5 abstracts regardless of presenting author, and may serve as presenting author on no more than 2 abstracts.
  • Presenting authors are required to register for the conference. Only registered presenting authors will be scheduled for presentation and included in the program; registration details and deadlines will be provided upon acceptance.
  • Submission of an abstract acknowledges your acceptance for it to be published in the official conference publications; all accepted abstracts will be published in a peer-reviewed journal.
  • The presenting author must ensure all co-authors are aware of the abstract's content and agree to its submission.
  • Abstracts presented at another scientific meeting within the previous 12 months are not eligible.
  • Submitters will be required to disclose any conflicts of interest.
  • If accepted for poster or oral presentation, you will receive further communication about presenting on the virtual platform.
  • Please do not submit multiple copies of the same abstract, and contact us if you have not received confirmation that your abstract was received.
Ready to submit? Email your abstract to asocconference@gmail.com with the subject "ASOC 2026 Abstract - [Presenting Author Last Name]" before the extended deadline of September 6, 2026.

What ASOC Covers

The full spectrum of stroke science

Prevention & Risk Factors

Hypertension, NCDs, and population-level strategies to stop stroke before it starts.

Acute Stroke Care

Stroke units, thrombolysis, and expanding access to time-critical treatment across African health systems.

Rehabilitation & Recovery

Neurorehabilitation, community reintegration, and life after stroke.

Epidemiology & Research

African-led data on stroke burden, genetics, and outcomes across the continent.

Policy & Advocacy

Translating evidence into national health policy and sustainable stroke systems.

Careers & Capacity

Mentorship, scholar presentations, and building the next generation of African stroke researchers.
